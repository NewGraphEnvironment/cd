# Code-check round 3: #137 northern BC vignette

Reviewer: round-3 subagent, 2026-10-14. Inputs: branch diff, round-2 fixes patch, `claims-enumeration.md`, bundled `inst/vignette-data/northern_bc.rds` (windows 1951–1980 / 2016–2025 / 2023–2025), rendered `docs/articles/northern-bc.html`. Nothing in the repo was edited apart from this file.

## Mechanism

Each sentence's backing check was written at the same time as the sentence, by whoever wrote the sentence, and it tests whatever statistic was to hand: almost always the **sign of one window mean**. It does not test what the words quantify. The page's quantifier words have no shared definition anywhere: "little", "less", "drier", "clearly", "the same", "most", "the last three [years]". So each claim gets its own threshold and its own unit of aggregation (a 3-year mean, a 10-year mean, a per-year count). Two consequences follow. First, sentences contradict each other on the page. A 4.6 % change is "little", a 3 % change is "less snow", and a 2.6 % change is "drier". Second, a claim about "the last three" years passes on a mean that one year (2023) carries alone, although the page tells the reader to look at the three years one at a time. `claims-enumeration.md` inherits the same blind spot because it is built from **sentences**: it checks that every sentence has *a* check, not that the check measures what the words say. Encodings that state a direction without words fall outside it: axis titles, scale limits, the scorecard tint rule. The data script even computes the right robustness check (`change_last3_without_first`, lines 101–105), but only for annual temperature, and nothing reads it.

Where the mechanism reaches: the intro (l.74–76), the scorecard caption and tint rule (l.156–175), the seasons heading, caption and facet label (l.217–233), the snow caption (l.256), the Bulkley closing sentence (l.352), the Bulkley temperature scale (l.335), the `claims` block and its tolerances (data script l.188–228, l.147–152), and CLAUDE.md's restated window.

## Findings

- **[bug] vignettes/northern-bc.Rmd:76 and :170–171 (`last3_drier`, data script l.207).** The intro says "Total rain and snow moved little over ten years, though the last three were drier", and the scorecard caption says "the last three were drier". The next intro sentence tells the reader that "the figures show each of the last three years on its own, not just their average", which invites a per-year reading. Per year, annual precipitation vs 1951–1980 was: BULK −16.9 / +2.2 / +2.6 %, PARS −14.9 / +4.6 / +2.7 %, NECR −19.4 / +2.0 / −16.0 %. In two of the three watersheds, 2 of the 3 years were **wetter** than normal, and the 3-year mean is negative only because of 2023. Without 2023 it is BULK +2.4 % and PARS +3.7 %. The claim tests `mean(last3) < 0`, which is an average where the sentence reads per year. It is also a weaker inequality than the words: PARS's −2.6 % sits inside the ±6 % band that `prcp_moved_little` uses to call a change "little" in the same sentence. The visible contradiction is in the scorecard's PARS cell, which reads "+5 / -3 %": the text calls the +5 "little" and the −3 "drier". The script's own `change_last3_without_first` is the right test, but it is applied only to annual tmean, where it is robust. Applied here, it flips the sign.

- **[fragile] vignettes/northern-bc.Rmd:219, seasons caption "over the last three years winters warmed most" (`winter_warmed_most_last3`, data script l.211).** This also rests on 2023. Per-year winter tmean change was BULK 5.2 / 2.8 / 1.7 and PARS 5.6 / 3.2 / 0.2 °C. Without 2023, **summer** warmed most in BULK (2.65 vs 2.25) and in PARS (1.95 vs 1.70). In 2025 winter was the *least*-warmed season in all three watersheds. The claim compares 3-year means, so it passes, while the small open circles drawn beside the sentence show the opposite for 2025.

- **[fragile] vignettes/northern-bc.Rmd:219, "winters … were wetter" (`winter_wetter_last3`, l.216). This is a round-2 fix that moved the defect one axis over.** Round 2 deleted "a little" from "a little wetter", which made the sentence stronger, although the check (`> 0`) did not get stricter. BULK's winter is +4.7 % over the last three years, inside the same ±6 % band the intro calls "moved little". By year, each watershed has one of its three winters drier (BULK 2024 −15.4 %, PARS 2024 −9.1 %, NECR 2025 −2.0 %).

- **[fragile] vignettes/northern-bc.Rmd:74–75 (intro, in bold) and the scorecard caption, "all three are clearly warmer, they hold less snow" (`smaller_snowpack`, `warmer_both`, l.190, l.198).** Both claims test only `< 0` / `> 0`. PARS peak snowpack over the last 10 years is −3.1 %, smaller than the +4.6 % the next sentence calls "moved little". Nothing tests "clearly": `warmer_both` passes at +0.01 °C. The page needs one magnitude rule for "changed" vs "moved little", applied to every signal. Or the 10-year snow claim can be hedged for the Parsnip.

- **[fragile] vignettes/northern-bc.Rmd:156 and :174–175, scorecard tint rule.** Orange is the sign of both windows ("warmer or drier in both"), with no magnitude. NECR total precipitation (−2 / −11 %) is tinted orange "drier" in the very table whose caption says rain and snow "moved little over ten years". The tint is an unworded claim that the enumeration never covers ("tint rule definitional"), and it uses a third threshold (0), different from the prose's 6 %.

- **[fragile] vignettes/northern-bc.Rmd:232, seasons facet label "Temperature\n°C warmer than normal".** The label is one-sided, while the temperature figure's label is two-sided ("warmer (+) or cooler (−)", l.213). No claim backs it for the plotted per-year circles: `every_season_warmer` tests only the 10-year means. PARS winter 2025 is already +0.2 °C. A regen with one cool season-year plots a point reading "−0.3 °C warmer than normal". The label needs either a check on every plotted point or the two-sided wording.

- **[fragile] vignettes/northern-bc.Rmd:335, Bulkley temperature-change scale `limits = c(0, max(2.5, …))`.** Round 2 fixed censoring at the top and left it at the bottom. With `na.value = NA`, any cell below 0 °C renders transparent, so it reads as "no data" under a legend that says "°C warmer than normal". No assertion covers per-cell change: `bulkley_both_warmed` tests the two polygon means. The cell minimum is 1.77 °C today, so this is latent. The same `minmax()` call can supply the lower limit, or the script can `stopifnot(min > 0)`.

- **[fragile] vignettes/northern-bc.Rmd:352, "The same warming and the same loss of snow" (data script l.148–149). This is a round-2 fix that kept the defect.** Round 2 narrowed *what* is "the same" but kept "the same" unqualified, while the sentence two lines earlier says "about the same". The backing tolerance on snowpack loss is an absolute 3 percentage points on shares of about 8–9 %. That would pass 6 % vs 9 % (a one-third relative difference) and print it as "the same loss of snow".

- **[fragile] CLAUDE.md (diff, Vignettes section): "It compares 2016–2025, not `cd_compare()`'s default 2015–2025."** This restates in prose a window that the data script derives from the catalog (`last_year - 9`). The first regen after the catalog gains 2026 makes CLAUDE.md wrong, and nothing checks it. The vignette itself renders its windows inline. CLAUDE.md should say "the last 10 complete years" rather than the years.
